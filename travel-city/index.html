      About Travel City

      Travel City lets you step through a portal from one city to the next. Explore 46 cities across China, the USA, East Asia, Southeast Asia, Europe, South America and Africa.

      Use the portal, the arrow buttons, your ← → keys or a swipe to travel. The arrow in the portal’s caption tucks it into the top-right corner so you can enjoy the photos; click the small portal to bring it back. Every city has its own link, so the browser’s Back button and shared links work too.
