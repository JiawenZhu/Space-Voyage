About Planet Jumping

Planet Jumping is an experimental cosmic traversal experience that lets voyagers seamlessly warp across planets in our solar system via dimensional viewport portals.

Rendered with screen-locked 2D canvas masking and fake 3D perspective distortion, each planetary gate reveals high-fidelity cosmic approach footage while tracking viewer motion.

Created by Ubernatural — exploring digital spatial boundaries, celestial orbital mechanics, and interplanetary design.
