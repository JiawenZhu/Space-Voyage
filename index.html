    About Space Voyage

    Space Voyage is an experimental journey across the inner Solar System. Step through the portal to warp from one planet to the next.

    Use the portal, the arrow buttons, your ← → keys or a swipe to travel. Every planet has its own link, so the browser’s Back button and shared links work too.

    Created by Ubernatural — exploring digital spatial boundaries, orbital mechanics and interplanetary design.
